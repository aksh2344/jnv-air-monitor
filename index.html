CAMPUS AIR QUALITY: SAFE & OPTIMAL
Atmospheric conditions at JNV Chandpur campus are clean and safe. MQ-135 gas concentration is within safe baseline limits and DHT22 thermal comfort is optimal.

Morning Assembly & Physical Training (P.E.)

When MQ-135 gas is Safe (<800 PPM) and Heat Index < 32°C, morning assembly, mass P.T., and playground sports proceed as usual. If Heat Index exceeds 38°C, outdoor activities shift to shaded or indoor covered areas with ample hydration.

Classrooms & Hostel Dormitories

Maintain continuous cross-ventilation during daylight hours. If MQ-135 gas levels enter Warning status (800–1200 PPM), windows and ventilators must be opened immediately to circulate fresh air and prevent indoor CO2 and odor buildup.

Science Labs & Campus Infirmary Protocol

Science laboratories utilize MQ-135 gas telemetry to detect hazardous chemical vapors (ammonia, benzene, alcohol, and smoke). The campus infirmary maintains ORS hydration packs for students during high Heat Index summer periods.

MQ-135 Gas & Air Quality Standards

Range (PPM) Category Atmospheric Status & Impact
< 800 PPM Safe Fresh outdoor and indoor air quality; minimal trace gases
800 - 1200 PPM Warning Moderate gas/CO2 concentration; classroom cross-ventilation advised
> 1200 PPM Hazardous Elevated volatile gases or smoke; inspect ventilation & sources immediately

Air Comfort (Heat Index) Scale

Heat Index Comfort Zone Physical Health Advisory
< 27°C (< 80°F) Comfortable Optimal thermal comfort; normal sports and campus routines
27°C - 32°C Caution Fatigue possible with prolonged physical activity in sun
32°C - 41°C Extreme Caution Heat cramps & exhaustion possible; hydrate regularly
> 41°C (> 105°F) Danger Heat exhaustion likely; suspend strenuous outdoor drills

PROJECT NAME

JNV Smart Air Monitor

Active IoT Node

JNV Smart Air Monitor is an IoT-based environmental monitoring application. It provides real-time tracking of ambient temperature and humidity levels using a DHT22 sensor and ESP microcontroller connected via Firebase. Designed to deliver high accuracy and seamless live data visualisations for campus and laboratory monitoring.
